ink-motion

Hand-drawn SVG that draws itself.

Constant pen speed, spring landing, scroll scrub. One small module, no dependencies, no build step.

01 — Draw once

Enters the viewport, draws every stroke at ~620 units per second, in DOM order.

02 — Scrub with scroll

Every stroke is tied to progress. Scroll down and it draws; scroll up and it un-draws.

progress 0%

03 — Spring landing

Dots and cards land with a damped spring (stiffness 180, damping 22) instead of a linear fade.
